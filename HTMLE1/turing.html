Alan Turing

Alan Turing was a British mathematician, computer scientist, and
cryptanalyst who is considered one of the fathers of modern computing.
During World War II, he played a major role in breaking the German/Nazi
Enigma code. He also developed important ideas about artificial
intelligence and theoretical computer science.
