Animals

Bear Lion Shark

Bears are large mammals known for their powerful bodies,
strong claws, and excellent sense of smell. They can live
in a variety of environments, including forests, mountains,
and Arctic regions.

Lions are large carnivorous mammals known for their strength
and social behavior. They are the only big cats that commonly
live and hunt in groups called prides.

Sharks are a diverse group of marine animals that have existed
for hundreds of millions of years. They have streamlined bodies,
sharp teeth, and highly developed senses that help them locate
prey in the ocean.
